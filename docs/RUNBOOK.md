# June & Bloom: Founders Runbook (`docs/RUNBOOK.md`)

Quick operational guide for the two founders. No developer is needed for daily operations.

---

## 1. Daily Order Fulfilment & Tracker (`/admin`)
1. Open `/admin` on your phone (installable to your home screen).
2. Under **Orders & Tracker**, tap **Tap to Advance Status** to move an order across `Received → Preparing → Ready → Out → Delivered`. The customer's private `/order/[id]` link updates immediately.
3. Tap **Packing Slip** on any order to print the single-page Packing Slip and Handwritten Linen Gift Card.
4. Open **Today & Run Sheet** to print the daily courier sheet grouped by GTA zone and time window, with allergies highlighted.

## 2. Adding a Gift Box or Adjusting Stock
1. Open `/admin` and select **Inventory & Products**.
2. Use the `+` and `-` buttons beside any box or charcuterie board to adjust stock on hand. When stock reaches `0`, the storefront automatically shows `Sold out`.
3. Use the **Add a New Gift Box** panel on the right to publish a new box with its price and 5–6 item list.

## 3. Pausing an Offer (`Switches, Not Deploys`)
1. Open `/admin` and select **Offer Switches**.
2. Toggle `gifting_open`, `charcuterie_open`, `styling_open`, or `takedown_storage_open`.
3. When paused, the public page immediately replaces checkout with a polite seasonal pause notice and concierge link.
